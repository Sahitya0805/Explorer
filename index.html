      Where the plan comes from

      Nothing here is made up. The sights and photos come from Wikipedia, the forecast from Open-Meteo, and the hotels and places to eat from OpenStreetMap. The budget is a rough split of what you enter, so treat it as a starting point, not a quote.

      • Sights within 10 km, a few per day, each linking to its Wikipedia page
      • Weather for each day of the trip, up to 16 days ahead
      • Stays and food nearby, with a Google Maps link to compare prices
      • A packing list based on that forecast
